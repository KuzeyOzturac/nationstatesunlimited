# Archive model contract
Schema version 2. src/catalogue.js is generated by scripts/import-archive.py. Do not hand-edit its source facts.

Each option retains its original number, outcome line, observed statistical means and policy transitions. Full min/max/mean/sample-count records are in src/data/issues-NN.json. The compact catalogue contains only nine explicitly mapped local indicators. A selected option adds its observed mean to each mapped local score, rounded to four decimals and clamped to 0–100. No invented coefficient or taper remains. Other source statistics are inspectable as evidence but are not added to unrelated indicators. Population does not grow per decision.

Definite additions/removals update the named-policy set. Conditional observations remain unresolved and visibly disclosed; they do not randomly enact laws. No effect is invented for an unavailable observation.

Eligibility is only enforced where a source-backed rule exists in src/eligibility.js. Unknown eligibility is marked as unknown and permitted in broad sandbox mode. Verified-only mode restricts to documented prerequisites. The twelve-decision cooldown is a local pacing rule and relaxes if the small verified pool is exhausted. A completely ineligible verified pool produces an actionable empty state.

Version 1 saves keep their country indicators and details. The original save is nested in legacyBackup. Fictional policy identifiers and fictional history are removed from active play; current archive history starts fresh. Government classifications remain simplified local labels, not the official 27-category matrix.
